# Content sources & practices

This app’s coaching and headset guidance reflects publicly shared **Alive Inside** and **Healing Headset / Memory Player** practices associated with filmmaker **Michael Rossato-Bennett** and the Alive Inside project. It is educational caregiver support — **not medical advice**.

## Practices embedded in the app

1. **Preferred personalized music over generic decade playlists**  
   Specific life-story songs (wedding song, hymns, dances, job-radio favorites) awaken identity more reliably than “anything from the 1940s.”

2. **Timing before hard moments**  
   Start preferred music **15–20 minutes before** ADLs (bathing, eating) and before expected **sundowning**.

3. **Engagement tool**  
   Stay present while listening. Do **not** leave someone alone with headphones for long stretches.

4. **Lingering effect**  
   Benefits of preferred music can linger roughly **~45 minutes** — a useful window for care tasks.

5. **Specificity**  
   Music Detective pushes for named artists, songs, dances, hymns, hometowns, jobs, and hobbies.

6. **Caregiver self-compassion**  
   Guilt and grief are framed as normal; caregivers deserve support.

7. **Headset loading**  
   Charge → build playlist → legal DRM-free MP3/WAV → microSD (USB with power ON or card reader) → drag files (order = play order) → listen together → troubleshoot (hold play for power; volume may skip; bump dots; hearing aids trial; alternate USB/reader).

## Crisis handling

If the user mentions suicide, self-harm, or harming an elder, the coach shows **988** / emergency guidance and **stops** coaching.
